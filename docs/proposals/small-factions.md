# Proposed small factions (for the canon doc)

**Status: PROPOSED.** None of this is canon until the DM adopts it. Each faction is anchored to real buildings and people from the DM City Index (census v20, buildings v20, factions v21), which is reference material. The *people and places* come from the index. Their *membership and roles in these factions* are proposals.

**Purpose:** local, low-stakes groups who cause most of the day-to-day friction for a small business. The 20 big factions (Cloverpot Compact, Deep Ledger, Merchants' Guild & Guild Wardens, City Guard and Watch, and others) stay in the background. They get involved only through the explicit escalation links below, which gives the "trouble cascades until someone intervenes" design a natural top rung.

A matching spreadsheet with the same columns as the factions workbook (plus a few extra) is in [`small-factions.csv`](small-factions.csv). IDs use the prefix `SF-` so they never collide with the workbook's `FAC-` IDs.

## SF-001 — The Ravenshade Lads

- **Kind:** street protection crew; 6–8 members.
- **Turf:** Ravenshade Way and Bramble Way, Commons.
- **Hangout:** The White Lantern, a tavern at 1 Ravenshade Way (AR-0478).
- **Leader:** **Rill Cloverpot** (P-01060), a 29-year-old halfling laborer. Lodges at 1 Greenbottle Lane and is already an active Cloverpot Compact member in the census.
- **Known members:** Nim Tumblefern (P-01003, 40, laborer) and Pella Tumblebrook (P-01000, 27, porter), both Compact members who live at 41 Ravenshade Way.
- **Public face:** "We keep an eye on the street."
- **Core activities:** collect weekly "street dues" from carts and stalls, run errands for the Compact, and lean on newcomers.
- **Use of force:** shoves, smashed carts, spoiled stock. Real violence needs the Compact's nod.
- **Relationships:** pay a cut to the Cloverpot Compact (FAC-002). Contempt for the Barrow Hands (SF-005), who share their street. Avoid the Watch.
- **Escalation:**
  1. Watchers.
  2. The ask.
  3. A smashed cart.
  4. A beaten seller.
  5. "Back dues" with interest.
  6. **The Compact itself sends someone.**
  It stops when real dues are paid, or when the party settles it in person (with the Lads, or above their heads with the Compact).
- **For the pie business:** the default protection-racket actor for a Commons business. Replaces the stand-in "Ninepenny Crew".
- **Table hooks:**
  - Rill wants to earn standing in the Compact. A newcomer who pays promptly makes him look good, and one who refuses makes him look weak.
  - The White Lantern's back room is where the dues ledger lives.

## SF-002 — The Lucky Die Ring

- **Kind:** unlicensed gaming-house crew; about 7 members.
- **Turf:** Petitioners' Lane and Copper Measure Lane, Grand Market.
- **Base:** The Lucky Die, a gaming house at 3 Petitioners' Lane (AR-0778).
- **Leader:** **Tarin Krail** (P-08473), 59, proprietor of The Lucky Die. Listed as informal work with a disreputable standing.
- **Known members:** the house staff, who all live at 5 Petitioners' Lane (use the IDs: these names recur across the census):
  - Ysra Lark (P-08466, runner)
  - Naren Brend (P-08479, keeps the books)
  - Celia Vell (P-08475, porter)
  - Elise Dale (P-08476, laborer)
  - Ansel Mere (P-08481, clerk)
  - Tomas Woodell (P-08482, assistant)
- **Public face:** a friendly game of dice, cash credit for regulars.
- **Core activities:** dice and cards, loans to stallholders at ruinous rates, a pickpocket line that fences through the market's pawnbrokers, and "insurance" for stalls.
- **Use of force:** debt collection by muscle; otherwise avoids attention.
- **Relationships:** tolerated by some Watch sergeants (FAC-006), and careful never to cross the Guild Wardens or the Deep Ledger.
- **Escalation:**
  1. Stall "insurance" offered.
  2. Pickpockets working the queue.
  3. A seller tempted into debt.
  4. The debt used as leverage ("let our runner work your cart").
  5. The debt sold to someone worse.
- **For the pie business:** the Grand Market crew. Replaces the stand-in "Saltgutter Crew". Ties naturally to staff integrity: a seller with gambling debts.
- **Table hooks:**
  - Naren Brend's ledgers know every stallholder's debts.
  - A **Beren Krail** (P-00036, an office guard at the Bureau of Sanitation) appears in a Crown Ward district prompt arguing that a sanitation permit isn't guild standing. Same surname as Tarin, but the census records no relation; any tie is the DM's call.

## SF-003 — The Washhouse Lane Bakers' Circle

- **Kind:** informal bakers' circle; four bakeries.
- **Turf:** Washhouse Lane, Redbrick Row and Street of Lanterns, Commons.
- **Meeting place:** Oven Bakehouse, 7 Washhouse Lane (AR-0462).
- **Convener:** **Nella Garrick** (P-05985), 42, proprietor of Oven Bakehouse; lives at 3 Loom Court, outside the walls.
- **Members:**
  - Cedren Kell (P-03584), The Warm Oven, 1 Washhouse Lane
  - Maren Rusk (P-01238), Honey Bun & Bread Bell, 22 Redbrick Row
  - Rhea Selden (P-01717), Rolling Pin & Rising Loaf, 15 Street of Lanterns
- **Public face:** neighborly bakers who share a flour order and keep bread prices "fair".
- **Core activities:** an informal price floor, shared bulk flour buying, closing ranks against newcomers, and a quiet word to the Guild Wardens about "irregular" sellers.
- **Use of force:** none. Gossip, boycotts, complaints.
- **Relationships:**
  - Defer to the Merchants' Guild & Guild Wardens (FAC-005).
  - Resent the Ravenshade Lads' street dues.
  - Buy their meat from the Redbrick Butchers (SF-004).
- **Escalation:**
  1. Bakers buy our pies to taste them.
  2. A price war.
  3. Whispers ("what's really in the magic cooks' pies?").
  4. A formal complaint to the Guild Wardens.
- **For the pie business:** the local rival and the first rung of guild pressure in the Commons. Replaces the stand-ins "Hobb's Hot Pies" and the "Bakers' and Piemakers' Fellowship".
- **Table hooks:**
  - Nella could become an ally: she wants a cheaper flour source, and the party might know one.
  - A truce means joining their price floor.

## SF-004 — The Redbrick Butchers

- **Kind:** butchers' price ring; three shops.
- **Turf:** Redbrick Row and Oatmarket Lane, Commons.
- **Meeting place:** The Iron Cleaver, 24 Redbrick Row (AR-0382).
- **Leader:** **Bera Barrelbrace** (P-01291), a 68-year-old dwarf, proprietor of The Iron Cleaver.
- **Members:**
  - Tovin Ordel (P-01288), The Ox Head, 26 Redbrick Row
  - Kael Kerrin (P-01503), The White Bone, 2 Oatmarket Lane; lives at 1 Oatmarket Lane
- **Public face:** honest meat at honest prices.
- **Core activities:** a shared meat price, first pick of carcasses for long-time customers, squeezing anyone who buys "foreign" meat.
- **Use of force:** none. Supply is the weapon.
- **Relationships:** Bera's kin include Kelda Barrelbrace (P-08067), proprietor of The Barley Sheaf bakery in Guildstrand and a Stonehome Heritage House member (FAC-003). The census lists no direct relation between them, so it's a possible family link for the DM to confirm.
- **Escalation:**
  1. Prices quietly rise for newcomers.
  2. "Sold out of good cuts today."
  3. Supply refused.
  4. Family ties used to bring in Stonehome pressure.
- **For the pie business:** the supply side. Exclusive deals, shortages and price hikes have a face. Pairs with the "exclusive supplier deal" decision.
- **Table hooks:**
  - Tovin Ordel shares a surname with Edrin Ordel, who leads the Barrow Hands (SF-005); the census lists no relation. If the DM makes them kin, the vendors and the butchers have a family feud.

## SF-005 — The Barrow Hands

- **Kind:** street vendors' mutual; dozens of loosely attached vendors. The census lists 76 vendors in the Commons and Grand Market.
- **Turf:** cart and barrow pitches across the Commons and Grand Market.
- **Meeting place:** The White Bell, a tavern at 12 Bramble Way (AR-0491).
- **Leader:** **Edrin Ordel** (P-00970), 63, a vendor living in the tenement at 34 Ravenshade Way (AR-0329).
- **Known member:** Calder Pike (P-00962), 62, a vendor in the same tenement.
- **Public face:** mutual aid for the people who sell from the street.
- **Core activities:** sharing good pitches, warning each other about Wardens and crews, a bail and repair fund, cheap informal "membership" instead of guild dues.
- **Use of force:** none. Numbers, noise, and knowing everyone.
- **Relationships:**
  - At odds with the Guild Wardens (FAC-005) over unlicensed trading.
  - Uneasy neighbors of the Ravenshade Lads, who live on the same street and tax the same carts.
- **Escalation:** this is a potential **ally**. Joining is a cheap alternative to the guild (pitch sharing, early warnings) that annoys the Wardens and the Bakers' Circle.
- **For the pie business:** a new choice for a cart business. Also a natural hiring pool for sellers.
- **Table hooks:**
  - Edrin knows which corners sell, and which corners the Lads watch.
  - The Hands could ask the party to stand up to the Lads on everyone's behalf.

## SF-006 — The Dawnfire Row Residents' Committee

- **Kind:** neighbors' association; about six households.
- **Turf:** the Guildstrand end of Dawnfire Promenade, **the party's own street**.
- **Meeting place:** 30 Dawnfire Promenade (AR-0610), the townhouse of the committee's secretary.
- **Leader:** **Dara Dain** (P-02131), 57. She works as registrar at the Guildhall of Smiths next door and knows how to write a petition.
- **Members:**
  - the Marrows (13 Dawnfire, AR-0601)
  - Orla Ironledger and Perrin Lark (22, AR-0602)
  - the Pentons (26, AR-0604)
  - the Perricks (28, AR-0606)
- **Neighbors of note:** the party's house (17, AR-0603) sits between the **Guildhall of Smiths** (19, AR-0605; hall steward Fara Tanner, P-02088) and the **Guildhall of Carters and Drovers** (21, AR-0594; Ketta Coalbraid, P-02006, a shopkeeper, lives there).
- **Public face:** keeping Dawnfire Promenade respectable.
- **Core activities:** complaints about smoke, noise, early deliveries, cart traffic and "dangerous magic", made through petitions to the landlord, the guildhalls and the Watch.
- **Use of force:** none. Paperwork and gossip.
- **Relationships:** friendly with the guildhall stewards next door. Their complaints carry weight with the Guild Wardens (FAC-005) and the Watch (FAC-006).
- **Escalation:**
  1. Raised eyebrows.
  2. A polite note.
  3. A petition.
  4. A complaint to the landlord (lease trouble).
  5. A complaint to the Watch or the licensing office.
- **For the pie business:** the home-front risk for running a kitchen in a rented row house. It matters most for kitchen expansion (living-room workshop, more deliveries) and any construct incident.
- **Table hooks:**
  - The DM ruled that the households the index lists at No. 17 (the Tanners and Harrowells) don't live there. Fara Tanner, the hall steward next door, shares the Tanner surname. *Optional:* the displaced Tanners are her kin, and she resents the newcomers.
  - Winning over Dara Dain makes the whole street easier.

## SF-007 — The Stewards' Table

- **Kind:** servants' gossip circle; senior stewards of High March households. The census lists 42 stewards in High March.
- **Turf:** the back doors and kitchens of High March.
- **Meeting place:** rotates between households. A suggested regular host is **Harlan Hollis** (P-00449), 69, steward, 4 Ministry Row.
- **Known members:**
  - Roder Kerrin (P-00286), 71, 12 Ministry Row
  - Kelda Kegward (P-00446), 65, a dwarf, 42 Silvergate Terrace
- **Public face:** none; it's private.
- **Core activities:**
  - Decide which tradespeople get through the back door.
  - Recommend or blacklist suppliers.
  - Trade household news.
- **Use of force:** none. One or two junior members sell news of empty houses to burglars. They're deliberately unnamed, so the DM can decide.
- **Relationships:** in practice, serve the Silvergate Circle's households (FAC-009). Lord Roder Kell (P-00291), a Silvergate member, is also listed as a steward.
- **Escalation:** this works through reputation. Praise opens doors and blacklists close them, and a loose tongue can bring burglars.
- **For the pie business:** the gatekeepers for a luxury High March business, covering word of mouth, delivery access and the High March burglary risk. Replaces the stand-in "Velvet Gloves".
- **Table hooks:**
  - Harlan Hollis can make a reputation in a single supper.
  - Roder Kerrin shares a surname with Kael Kerrin of the Redbrick Butchers (SF-004); the census lists no relation.

2. **Family links marked "possible"** are not in the census. Confirm or drop them.
3. **Stand-ins these replace** in the simulation (once wired in):

   | Stand-in | Replaced by |

   | Ninepenny Crew | SF-001 The Ravenshade Lads |
   | Saltgutter Crew | SF-002 The Lucky Die Ring |
   | Bakers' and Piemakers' Fellowship | SF-003 locally, and the Guild Wardens (FAC-005) at the top |
   | Hobb's Hot Pies | SF-003 member bakeries |
   | Velvet Gloves | SF-007's loose tongues plus unnamed burglars |

   Grand Market and High March rival bakeries still need choosing from the index.
4. Nothing here changes the City Index itself.
